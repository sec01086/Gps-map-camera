Add the location, address, time — and logo, if turned on — to photos already in your gallery, even ones not taken with this app. If a photo already has GPS info saved in it (most phone camera photos do), that location and date are read automatically; otherwise you'll be asked to enter one. Works with JPEG/PNG; HEIC photos (common on iPhone) usually can't be read by the browser — export them as JPEG first if needed.
